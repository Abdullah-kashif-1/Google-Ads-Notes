# Search Ads (High Intent Engine)

## What are Search Ads?
Text-based ads shown on search engine results when users type queries.

## Where They Appear
- Google Search Results
- Search Partners

## Why They Matter
Users already have intent → highest conversion probability.

---

## Key Features
- Keyword targeting
- Match types (Broad, Phrase, Exact)
- Ad extensions (sitelinks, callouts, etc.)

---

## Best Use Cases
- Lead generation
- Service-based businesses
- High-intent product searches

---

## Advantages
- High ROI potential
- Immediate traffic
- Strong conversion rates

---

## Limitations
- Expensive in competitive niches
- Requires keyword strategy

---

## Pro Strategy (Think10x)
- Focus on "buyer intent keywords"
- Use SKAG or thematic ad groups
- Optimize for Quality Score

---

## Expert Tip
Don't target traffic. Target intent.
