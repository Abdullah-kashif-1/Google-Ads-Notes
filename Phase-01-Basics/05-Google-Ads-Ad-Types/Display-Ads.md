# Display Ads (Attention Builder)

## What are Display Ads?
Visual banner/image ads shown across websites, apps, and Google Display Network.

## Where They Appear
- Blogs
- News websites
- Mobile apps

---

## Purpose
Top-of-funnel awareness + retargeting

---

## Targeting Options
- Interests
- Demographics
- Custom audiences
- Remarketing

---

## Best Use Cases
- Brand awareness
- Retargeting visitors
- Visual storytelling

---

## Advantages
- Massive reach
- Low cost per impression
- Strong brand recall

---

## Limitations
- Lower intent
- Banner blindness

---

## Pro Strategy (ThinkLonger)
- Use retargeting sequences
- Combine with Search Ads
- Focus on creatives > targeting

---

## Expert Tip
Display doesn’t convert cold traffic. It warms it.
