# 🔍 Search Intent (Informational vs Transactional)

## 📌 Introduction

Most people focus only on **keywords** when running Google Ads…
But they ignore the most important factor: **Search Intent**

👉 This is the main reason why ads get clicks but fail to convert.

---

## 🧠 What is Search Intent?

Search intent is the **reason behind a user’s search query**.

In simple words:
👉 *What does the user actually want?*

Understanding intent helps you:

* Show the right ad to the right person
* Improve conversion rates
* Avoid wasting budget

---

## 🔑 Why Search Intent Matters

* Not every search means the user wants to buy
* Some users are just researching
* Some are ready to take action

👉 If you target the wrong intent:

* You get clicks ❌
* But no conversions ❌

---

## 🔍 Types of Search Intent

In Google Ads (basic level), we focus on two main types:

1️⃣ Informational Intent
2️⃣ Transactional Intent

---

## 🔹 1. Informational Intent (Learning Stage)

### 📌 Definition

The user wants to **learn or get information**.

### 🔍 Examples

* what is Google Ads
* how to run ads
* best marketing strategies

### 🧠 User Mindset

* Curious
* Exploring options
* Not ready to buy yet

---

### ✅ Characteristics

* Long, question-based searches
* Low buying intent
* High traffic potential

---

### 🎯 Best Strategy

* Use blog content / guides
* Create educational posts
* Build trust and awareness

👉 Goal: **Educate, not sell**

---

### ⚠️ Common Mistake

Running **sales ads** on informational keywords

👉 Result:

* High clicks
* Low conversions
* Wasted budget

---

## 🔹 2. Transactional Intent (Buying Stage)

### 📌 Definition

The user wants to **take action or make a purchase**.

### 🔍 Examples

* hire Google Ads expert
* buy running shoes online
* best PPC agency near me

### 🧠 User Mindset

* Ready to act
* Comparing options
* Looking for the best deal

---

### ✅ Characteristics

* Action-based keywords
* High conversion potential
* Lower but more valuable traffic

---

### 🎯 Best Strategy

* Run conversion-focused ads
* Use strong CTAs (Call to Action)
* Optimize landing pages

👉 Goal: **Convert the user**

---

## ⚖️ Informational vs Transactional

| Factor            | Informational | Transactional     |
| ----------------- | ------------- | ----------------- |
| User Goal         | Learn         | Buy / Take Action |
| Intent Level      | Low           | High              |
| Conversion Chance | Low           | High              |
| Strategy          | Educate       | Sell              |

---

## 💡 Pro Strategy (Real-World Use)

### 🔹 Step 1: Identify Intent

Analyze your keywords and classify them:

* Informational
* Transactional

---

### 🔹 Step 2: Align Strategy

* Informational → Content marketing
* Transactional → Paid ads / sales funnel

---

### 🔹 Step 3: Funnel Thinking

* Top of Funnel → Informational
* Bottom of Funnel → Transactional

👉 Move users from learning → buying

---

## 🚀 Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ Ignoring search intent
❌ Using only high-traffic keywords
❌ Selling too early
❌ Not matching landing page with intent
❌ No funnel strategy

---

## 🎯 Final Thoughts

Keywords bring traffic…
But **intent brings conversions**

👉 The real power is in matching:
**Right Keyword + Right Intent + Right Strategy**

---

## 📈 Practical Insight

If your ads are getting clicks but no sales…
👉 You are likely targeting the wrong intent

Fix the intent… and results will follow 🚀
