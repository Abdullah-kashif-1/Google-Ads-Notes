# 🔑 What are Keywords + Types

## 📌 What are Keywords?

Keywords are the **words or phrases** that users type into Google.

👉 Your ads appear based on these keywords.

### Example:

* buy running shoes
* best shoes for jogging

👉 If your keyword matches → your ad shows
👉 If not → no results ❌

---

## ⚠️ Why Keywords Matter

* Target the right audience
* Control ad visibility
* Improve conversions
* Save budget

👉 No right keywords = No results

---

## 🧠 Types of Keywords

Google Ads has 3 main match types:

1️⃣ **Broad Match**
2️⃣ **Phrase Match**
3️⃣ **Exact Match**

👉 Each type controls how your ads are triggered.
