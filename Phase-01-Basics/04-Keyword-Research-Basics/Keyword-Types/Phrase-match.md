## 🔹 Phrase Match (Balanced)

### 📌 Definition

Your ad shows when the search includes your keyword phrase.

### 🔍 Example

Keyword: "running shoes"

Your ad may show for:

* buy running shoes
* running shoes for men

---

### ✅ Advantages

* Balanced reach + control
* Relevant traffic

### ❌ Disadvantages

* Less reach than broad

### 🎯 Best Use

* Recommended for most campaigns
