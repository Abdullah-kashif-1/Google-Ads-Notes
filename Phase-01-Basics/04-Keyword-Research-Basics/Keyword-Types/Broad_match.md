# 🔓 Broad Match (Maximum Reach)

## 📌 Definition

Your ad shows on **related searches**, even if the exact keyword is not used.

## 🔍 Example

Keyword: running shoes

Your ad may show for:

* sports shoes
* jogging sneakers
* best shoes for running

---

## ✅ Advantages

* Maximum reach
* Discover new keywords

## ❌ Disadvantages

* Less control
* Irrelevant traffic
* Budget waste risk

---

## 🎯 Best Use

* Keyword research
* Testing new ideas

⚠️ Not recommended for beginners without strategy
