## 🔹 Exact Match (High Intent)

### 📌 Definition

Your ad shows only for exact or close meaning searches.

### 🔍 Example

Keyword: [running shoes]

Your ad may show for:

* running shoes
* shoes for running

---

### ✅ Advantages

* High intent audience
* Better conversions
* Full control

### ❌ Disadvantages

* Low traffic

### 🎯 Best Use

* High-converting campaigns

---

## 💡 Pro Tip

Start with:
👉 Phrase Match + Exact Match

Then scale using Broad Match smartly 🚀
