# 📊 Google Ads Account Setup  

A strategic guide to setting up and configuring a Google Ads account for scalable and performance-driven campaign execution 🚀  

---

## 🔹 Account Creation  

Establishing a properly structured Google Ads account is the foundation of any successful paid marketing strategy.  

### Process:  

1. Access the Google Ads platform  
2. Initiate account setup  
3. Authenticate using a Google account  
4. Define primary business objective (leads, sales, traffic)  
5. Configure core business details (website, location, audience targeting)  

👉 For full campaign control and advanced configurations, switch to **Expert Mode**  

---

## 🔹 Billing Configuration 💳  

An optimized billing setup ensures smooth campaign delivery without interruptions.  

### Steps:  

1. Navigate to **Billing Settings**  
2. Select billing country and currency 🌍  
3. Choose preferred payment method:  
   - Credit/Debit Card  
   - Bank Transfer  
4. Define budget allocation approach  
5. Verify and activate billing profile  

---

## 🎯 Key Considerations  

✔️ Account creation is free; costs apply only on user interaction  
✔️ Billing configuration is mandatory before campaign activation  
✔️ Budget strategy plays a critical role in campaign efficiency  

---

## 🚀 Best Practices  

👉 Start with controlled budget allocation for initial testing  
👉 Monitor performance metrics (CTR, CPC, conversions) consistently  
👉 Ensure billing accuracy to prevent campaign disruption  
👉 Align campaign goals with clear KPIs
