# 📊 Google Ads Account Setup  

A structured guide to setting up and configuring a Google Ads account for effective campaign execution 🚀  

---

## 🔹 Account Creation  

Setting up a Google Ads account is the first step toward running scalable paid campaigns.  

### Process:  

1. Navigate to Google Ads platform  
2. Initiate account creation  
3. Authenticate via Google account  
4. Define business objective (leads, sales, traffic)  
5. Configure business details (website, location, targeting)  

👉 For advanced control, switch to **Expert Mode** during setup  

---

## 🔹 Billing Configuration 💳  

A properly configured billing system ensures uninterrupted campaign delivery.  

### Steps:  

1. Access **Billing Settings**  
2. Select billing country and currency 🌍  
3. Choose payment method:  
   - Credit/Debit Card  
   - Bank Transfer  
4. Define budget allocation strategy  
5. Confirm and activate billing profile  

---

## 🎯 Key Considerations  

✔️ Account setup is free; charges apply only on ad interaction  
✔️ Billing must be configured before campaign activation  
✔️ Budget planning directly impacts campaign performance  

---

## 🚀 Best Practices  

👉 Start with controlled budget allocation for testing  
👉 Continuously monitor spend vs performance  
👉 Ensure accurate billing details to avoid disruptions
